For you, always ♡

I don't think I could ever fit everything I feel for you into one little letter, but I still wanted to try. When I think about us, I think about all those ordinary moments that somehow became some of my favourite memories. Spending time with your family, being around my brothers, watching movies together, and staying up talking until two or three in the morning are things I will always remember. They were never just random days to me. They became little pieces of my life that I would happily go back to again and again.

I especially love remembering the evenings when we would sing together. I could honestly listen to you sing the same song a million times and still want to hear it one more time. And then there was cooking together, although let's be honest, I mostly stood there watching you and taking videos because you never really let me do much work anyway. 😭 I loved those moments though. I loved watching you, laughing with you, and keeping little videos because apparently I just really like having proof of my favourite person being cute.

You were also someone who was there for me whenever I needed you, except for those moments when you were busy, obviously. And I still remember the flowers. Whenever we went somewhere and you saw flowers, you would pick one and give it to me. Sometimes you would find those tiny sunflower-like flowers and pick six or seven of them, making the smallest little bouquet. It sounds like such a tiny thing, but those little flowers meant so much to me. They were never really just flowers. They were your way of thinking of me, and I don't think you ever realised how much I adored that.

I still remember that first bouquet too — the yellow flowers, the purple ones whose name I still don't know, the pink rose, and the hibiscus. I remember it because it was the first time I received flowers from you, and that made it special in a way I can't really explain. I hope you know that I notice those little things. I remember them. I keep them somewhere in my heart even when the moment itself has already passed.

And yes, before I become way too emotional, there is one extremely important issue that needs to be addressed. I have asked you for cheesecake for FOUR MONTHS. 😭 Four. Whole. Months. And somehow the cheesecake has still not appeared. I am choosing to believe this is simply a very long-term birthday present that is currently experiencing shipping delays.

But jokes aside, no matter who you become, where life takes you, or how much things change, I hope you always remember that you mattered to me deeply. I care about you, I adore you, and I am grateful for all the memories we have made. I don't know what every future day will look like, but I know that the memories we already have will always mean something to me.

So today, I just hope you have the kind of birthday that makes you genuinely happy. I hope this year is kind to you. I hope you take care of yourself, stay safe, laugh a lot, make beautiful memories, and become everything you want to become. And whenever you look back at this little website, I hope you remember that someone somewhere was smiling while making every tiny part of it for you.

Happy birthday, Carino. ♡

And I am STILL waiting for my cheesecake. 😭🤍
